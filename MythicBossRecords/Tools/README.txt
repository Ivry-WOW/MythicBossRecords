Mythic Boss Records - tools
===========================

Double-click "MBR Tools.cmd" (in the MythicBossRecords folder):

 1. Turn on automatic - do this once. Every 15 minutes (and when Windows starts) it:
      - reads the NEW part of your WoW\Logs\WoWCombatLog.txt and saves the boss DPS of
        +9 keys and higher in LocalLogData.lua. The addon loads it at login and shares it
        with everyone who has the addon, so your old logs fill the database for all.
      - installs a new version of the addon when one is released.
 2. Import my combat log now.
 3. Update the addon now.
 4. Read my whole combat log again.
 5. Turn off automatic.

Nothing is uploaded anywhere: your log is read on your PC only.
In WoW, type /combatlog before a key so the game writes the log.
Log of what the tools did: %LOCALAPPDATA%\MythicBossRecords\tools.log
